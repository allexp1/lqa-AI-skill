# English by country

Each country uses one dictionary consistently: the Canadian Oxford Dictionary (CA), the Macquarie or Australian Oxford (AU, per the Australian Government Style Manual), and the Oxford or Collins dictionaries (GB, IE, NZ). The script checks the high-confidence spellings below. Vocabulary and school terms are part of the linguistic pass.

## Spelling

| | en-US | en-CA | en-GB / en-IE | en-AU | en-NZ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| colour, favourite, behaviour | -or | **-our** | -our | -our | -our |
| centre, theatre | -er | **-re** | -re | -re | -re |
| organise / organize | -ize | **-ize** | -ise (‑ize accepted; never mixed) | **-ise** | -ise |
| analyse | analyze | **analyze** | analyse | analyse | analyse |
| travelled, cancelled | single l | **double l** | double l | double l | double l |
| defence, catalogue, pyjamas, jewellery | US forms | **Commonwealth** | Commonwealth | Commonwealth | Commonwealth |
| grey | gray | grey | grey | grey | grey |
| aluminium | aluminum | **aluminum** | aluminium | aluminium | aluminium |
| program / programme | program | program | programme (program = software) | **program** | programme |
| practice (noun) / practise (verb) | practice for both | **split** | split | split | split |
| math / maths | math | **math** | maths | maths | maths |
| mom / mum | mom | **mom** | mum | mum | mum |

Bold marks where a locale breaks the obvious US/UK pattern. Official US institution names keep their own spelling in every locale (Department of Defense).

## Conventions

| | en-US | en-CA | en-GB / en-IE | en-AU | en-NZ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Date | MM/DD/YYYY, "March 12" | Varies: prefer an unambiguous "12 March 2026" or ISO | DD/MM/YYYY, "12 March" | DD/MM/YYYY | DD/MM/YYYY |
| Quotes | "double" | "double" | 'single' common (GOV.UK and most publishers use double; follow the house) | 'single' (Style Manual) | 'single' common |
| Currency | $ USD | $ CAD (write C$ next to US$) | £ / € (IE) | $ AUD (A$ if ambiguous) | $ NZD (NZ$ if ambiguous) |
| Units | US customary in daily life; metric in science | metric (some imperial in daily speech) | metric; miles and pints in the UK | metric | metric |

Te reo Māori words in en-NZ keep their macrons (Māori, Tāmaki Makaurau) and take no English plural -s.
